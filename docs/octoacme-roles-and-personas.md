# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance / QA Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads define testing strategies, establish quality standards, and ensure all deliverables meet acceptance criteria and quality gates before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test plans and acceptance criteria validation strategies
- Set up and maintain automated testing infrastructure
- Conduct manual testing, regression testing, and exploratory testing
- Manage quality gates and sign-off on releases
- Identify and document quality risks
- Collaborate with developers on testability improvements

### Goals
- Ensure zero defects reach production (or acceptable quality thresholds)
- Reduce cycle time by catching issues early
- Provide data-driven quality metrics to inform release decisions

### Typical Communication
- Participation in sprint planning and acceptance criteria discussions
- Test reports and quality dashboards
- Release readiness sign-off

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide architectural vision, technical guidance, and design review to ensure solutions meet non-functional requirements and scale efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Lead technical design discussions and architecture reviews
- Mentor developers on design patterns and best practices
- Identify and mitigate technical risks
- Ensure solutions meet scalability, security, and performance requirements
- Guide technology selection and technical debt management
- Collaborate with Product and Project Managers on feasibility

### Goals
- Deliver maintainable, scalable systems
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Ensure consistent quality across the codebase

### Typical Communication
- Technical design documents and architecture reviews
- Technical risk registers and mitigation plans
- Regular guidance and code review feedback

---

## Stakeholder / Business Sponsor

### Role Summary
Business Sponsors represent organizational priorities, provide executive oversight, and ensure projects align with strategic goals and resource constraints.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charter and success metrics
- Provide executive sponsorship and remove organizational blockers
- Approve budget and resource allocation
- Participate in major decision gates
- Communicate project status to senior leadership
- Escalate risks and conflicts that require executive intervention

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value
- Maintain alignment with organizational strategy
- Minimize resource conflicts and delays

### Typical Communication
- Monthly executive status updates
- Milestone approval gates
- Escalation and risk reviews

---

## DevOps / Release Engineer

### Role Summary
Release Engineers manage CI/CD pipelines, automate deployment processes, and maintain production observability to ensure reliable, frequent releases.

### Responsibilities
- Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines
- Automate deployment and rollback processes
- Manage infrastructure and environment configuration
- Monitor production systems and incident response
- Ensure security scanning and compliance checks in pipelines
- Document deployment and runbooks

### Goals
- Enable continuous, low-risk deployments
- Maintain high availability and system reliability
- Reduce mean time to recovery (MTTR)

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and deployment windows
- Incident response coordination
- Infrastructure and pipeline documentation

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, coach teams on agile practices, and remove impediments to maintain team velocity and psychological safety.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove blockers and impediments
- Coach teams on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Maintain sprint metrics and burn-down tracking
- Facilitate communication between development and cross-functional teams
- Foster psychological safety and team engagement

### Goals
- Maintain consistent sprint velocity and team morale
- Improve team collaboration and communication
- Enable continuous process improvement

### Typical Communication
- Agile ceremonies and facilitation
- Impediment logs and escalation tracking
- Retrospective action items and team feedback
